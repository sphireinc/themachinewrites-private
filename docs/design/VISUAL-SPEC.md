# Approved homepage visual specification

Reference: [`approved-homepage.png`](approved-homepage.png), 1222 × 1287.

The reference is authoritative. Do not replace its structure or add marketing copy.

## Geometry and zones

- Canvas: white/near-white with a top horizontal rule beneath the masthead and navigation.
- Header: masthead at left; navigation and moon control at right. No tagline.
- Desktop grid at 1222px: narrow left filter rail, wide central timeline, narrow right information rail. Hairline vertical rules separate columns.
- Timeline: a vertical hairline with small blue event dots; date/time at left; type label, title, excerpt and compact metadata to the right.
- Right rail: pale-gray activity panel, three compact counts, topic pills, and a short About block with one blue “Learn more” link.
- Bottom space remains intentionally sparse.

## Visual tokens

- Background/white: `#FFFFFF`
- Pale surface: `#F3F6FA`
- Primary blue accent: `#1672F4`
- Near-black ink: `#172435`
- Slate secondary: `#526477`
- Divider: `#D8E1EB`
- Typography: restrained editorial/serif for headlines, monospace accent for masthead and metadata, neutral sans for body.

## Acceptance

Capture at 1222 × 1287 with deterministic fixture content. Compare zone boundaries, header placement, three-column widths, blue dots, spacing, typography hierarchy and restrained palette. Minor browser font rasterization differences are acceptable; structural redesign, invented copy and promotional sections are not.
